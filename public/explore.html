A sequence is not a structure.
The sequence builder uses chosen backbone torsions and fixed bond lengths and angles. Its shape follows those settings, rather than inferring a sequence-specific native fold. Proline, glycine and other residue-specific constraints are not enforced.
To examine a folded protein, open existing coordinates from a database or a file you already have. Predicted models from another tool can also be opened as coordinate files; Conforma does not verify their confidence or turn them into experimental evidence.
